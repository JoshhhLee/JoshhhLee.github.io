Hello — 初めまして

I am currently a MPhil students at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, working as a Graduate Research Assistant at the Photonics Research Center. Previously, I received my B.Sc in Physics (Electronics Instrumentation) from Universitas Negeri Semarang, Indonesia.

I also serve as a Research Assistant at BRIN (National Research and Innovation Agency, Indonesia), Research Center for Photonics — Micro-Opto-Electronics and Microsystems group — where I focus on developing a GUI for object segmentation using YOLOv11 and SAM-2, and on smart weapon development.

My research focuses on Image Processing, Python-based Software Development, ML and DL applications for instrumentation systems, and Optical Fiber Sensor simulation and fabrication.
