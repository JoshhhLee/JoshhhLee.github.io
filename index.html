About

Background & research

I am currently a PhD student in Electrical Engineering at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, majoring in Computer Vision and Image Processing, as an awardee of the UTM ASCEND Bright Talent International Scholarship. Before that, I completed my MPhil at UTM (Mechatronics Engineering, majoring in Control Systems and Optoelectronics), where I was a Graduate Lecture Assistant at the Photonics Research Center. I received my B.Sc in Physics (Instrumentation Electronics) from Universitas Negeri Semarang, Indonesia.

From 2024 to 2026 I was a Research Assistant at BRIN (National Research and Innovation Agency, Indonesia), Research Center for Photonics (Micro-Opto-Electronics and Microsystems group), where I developed a GUI for object segmentation using YOLOv11 and SAM-2, and worked on smart weapon development.

My PhD research focuses on multispectral imaging and deep learning for camouflaged object detection and segmentation. Before that, during my MPhil and at BRIN, I worked on Python-based software development, ML and DL applications for instrumentation systems, and optical fiber sensor simulation and fabrication.

Outside the lab, I enjoy working in large, interdisciplinary teams, from rocket avionics to physics STEM modules, and I am always curious to learn things outside my own field.

Teaching & learning

Tools I build for students

Each one is meant to be opened in a lecture, played with, and argued about, not just read.

Interactive series

The Lab Series

Two series of interactive labs, each one a single page you open and play with, with your own images. No install, no notebook, no account.

  • Image Processing: seven labs following Gonzalez & Woods (pixels & colour, histograms, spatial and frequency-domain filtering, restoration, morphology, and edges & segmentation)
  • Computer Vision: convolution, segmentation and encoder-decoders, from the sliding window up to camouflaged object detection
Open the series Free study tool

IELTS Study Kit

A free IELTS course in one page, made for high school students who don’t have access to IELTS books. Explanations in English and Bahasa Indonesia. Works on a phone.

  • Grammar: a 145-unit course with practice questions
  • Vocabulary: flashcards, quizzes and IELTS topic packs
  • Reading & Listening: a paraphrase trainer and spelling dictation
  • Writing: an essay vocabulary checker
