The Lab Series

Two series of labs. Image Processing covers the classical toolkit, following the textbooks most courses use: histograms, filters, the frequency domain, morphology. Computer Vision builds a convolutional network from the sliding window up to the problem I actually work on: finding animals that do not want to be found. Each lab is a single page you open and play with. No install, no notebook, no account.

Image Processing

Follows Gonzalez & Woods, Digital Image Processing (4th edition), section by section, with Burger & Burge, Digital Image Processing: An Algorithmic Introduction, alongside. Every lab takes your own image: drop one in and turn the dials.
